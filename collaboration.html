Independent testing & collaboration

Test the prediction, not the affiliation.

Agreement with the SDC framework as a whole is not required. Individual predictions can be examined independently, using existing experimental methods and discipline-specific standards.

Areas suitable for independent testing

  • Transient digital splay and plantar contact behaviour
  • Three-dimensional whole-limb kinematics
  • High-output locomotion and distal-contact propulsion
  • Terrain complexity and gait adaptation
  • Cross-species comparisons of locomotor architecture

Preferred collaboration format

Discrete, falsifiable predictions are preferred over endorsement of the entire framework. A laboratory may test one prediction, publish its own analysis, and reach its own conclusion.
