Research stance

SDC is presented as a theoretical programme: explicit mechanisms, cross-species consistency, concrete predictions, and conditions under which those predictions can fail.

Observe first.

Begin with locomotor behaviour, terrain, contact patterns, and system-level consequences.

Reconstruct the system.

Treat local structures as coupled components rather than isolated diagnoses or single-joint problems.

Test the prediction.

Independent testing, replication, falsification, and empirical disagreement are explicitly welcome.
